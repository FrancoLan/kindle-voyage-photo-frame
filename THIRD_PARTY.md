# Third-party software and services

This repository does not bundle jailbreak packages, FBInk, KindleForge/KPM, or proprietary Amazon/Apple components.

- [WinterBreak2](https://github.com/KindleModding/Winterbreak2) was used to prepare the tested Kindle.
- [FBInk](https://github.com/NiLuJe/FBInk) renders images and text on the E Ink framebuffer.
- KindleForge/KPM provides document-based script launching on the tested device.
- Apple iCloud Shared Albums supply photo records and assets.
- Apple MapKit performs reverse geocoding on the Mac.

The included `kindle-xcontrols` and `kindle-evgrab-cat` ARM binaries are built from the assembly source in `implementation/kindle/native` and are covered by this repository's MIT license.
